Here is the mistake, and it is made by almost everyone in this mode who can afford to make it: you get a nice one-bedroom on a quiet block, because after whatever you just came out of, quiet sounds like the thing you need.

Six months later you have a very pleasant apartment and no one to be in it with. The quiet block gave you nothing to walk past. The one-bedroom gave you no one to run into in a kitchen. And because nothing in your day forces a low-stakes interaction, every single social contact has to be scheduled by you, in advance, from zero. That is an exhausting way to build a life and most people stop doing it around week seven.

### Choose friction

Everything in your housing decision should be pointed at one goal: reduce the activation energy between you and other people.

**Roommates.** The single highest-leverage choice available. Not because roommates become your friends — sometimes they do, often they do not — but because they have their own friends, their own routines, and their own Tuesday nights, and proximity to that is a live wire into a social world you did not have to build. Boston's housing stock is full of three- and four-bedroom apartments precisely because the region is organised around this.

**A busy street.** If you live alone, live somewhere with life on the ground floor. The difference between a block you walk down with intent and a block where you can plausibly stop is the difference between a neighborhood you live in and one you sleep in.

**A building with a lobby.** Lower-yield than roommates but real, especially if it has a gym, a courtyard, or a laundry room where people linger.

### Sign short

Whatever your instinct is about lease length, go shorter. You do not yet know which part of this metro you belong in, and you are making the call with the least information you will ever have. Boston's lease cycle is heavily synchronized to September 1, which means an off-cycle lease can be awkward to line up — take the awkwardness. A year of reconnaissance followed by a deliberate second move beats three years of being slightly in the wrong place.

### And the part that is not about the apartment

Housing can only set the conditions. It cannot do the work. The apartment that makes this easiest is one you can leave at 7pm on a Wednesday without it being a whole production — because the actual answer to your mode is in the Friends section, and it is about showing up somewhere twice.
